# Item ideas

A shared list of items we might add. Anyone can add or change an idea through a pull request, like
any other change. When an item is being built, move it to "Next up" in the README; when it ships, it
leaves this list.

Nothing here is a promise to players, and the stats and powers are starting points, not decisions.

## Picked next

### Assassin's Creed: the Brotherhood (set)
A stealth set that rewards staying unseen.

- **Pieces:** hood, robes, legs, cape and hidden blades (dual knives worn on the wrists, using the
  Warglaives' two-hand code).
- **Bonuses:**
  - 2 pieces: faster, quieter sneaking that costs less stamina.
  - 3 pieces: **Eagle Vision**. While crouched, nearby creatures glow through walls (red if
    hostile, gold for a target worth hunting, such as a starred creature).
  - Full set: **Assassination** (a sneak attack on an unaware creature kills it; bosses lose a set
    share of their health instead) and **Leap of Faith** (no fall damage).
- **Detected:** once a creature is alerted to you, you can't assassinate it until it loses track of
  you.
- **Look:** retextured vanilla armour in the white and red Brotherhood style. The Fenris hood (a
  dark hood) is the lead candidate for the cowl; compare it with the Eitr-weave and Troll leather
  hoods in renders first.
- **Builds:** the first set with bonuses at 2, 3 and 4 pieces, which later sets can reuse.

## Favourites

### Mistborn (set)
- **Metals as trinkets:** each trinket is one metal, with a power while it burns and a flare when
  its adrenaline bar is full. One trinket slot means choosing your metal before you leave.

  | Metal | While burning | Flare |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tin | Clears darkness and mist around you | See through walls briefly |
  | Pewter | More strength, stamina and carry weight | Ignore stagger for a few seconds |
  | Steel | Higher, longer jumps | A huge launch where you're looking |
  | Iron | Pulls nearby loot to you | Yanks a creature towards you |
  | Copper | Hides you from detection | Nearby creatures lose track of you |
  | Brass | Calms creatures near you | Pacifies a group for a while |
  | Atium (endgame) | | A dodge window: a moment of seeing the future |

- **Metal vials:** meads made from Valheim's own metals that refill the bar.
- **Mistcloak** (cape): harder to see in mist, and the bar fills faster there. The Mistlands become
  the mist Mistborn are strongest in.
- **Obsidian daggers:** Mistborn fight with them, and Valheim has obsidian.

### Marauder's Map (Harry Potter)
Utility item. While equipped, the minimap shows named footprints for creatures and players in a
wide range, moving live. Opening it is "I solemnly swear...", closing it "Mischief managed". A
config setting decides whether other players show (an advantage in PvP).

### Gleipnir (Norse myth)
The ribbon that bound Fenrir, used like a Pokéball: throw it at a weakened creature (below about a
quarter of its health) and it's tamed at once. The myth makes it from six impossible things (a
cat's footfall, a woman's beard, a mountain's roots, a bear's sinews, a fish's breath, a bird's
spittle), which could map to ingredients from different biomes. Works on Valheim's tameable
creatures; a config setting could open it to others, at the risk of odd behaviour.

### Wolf-skins of the Völsungs (werewolf set)
Norse wolf-warriors (úlfheðnar) and the Völsunga saga's enchanted wolf-skins, built on vanilla's
Fenris armour and Flesh Rippers. When transformed (full adrenaline, or at night), you take the
Fenring's shape: faster, harder-hitting claws, no other weapons.

Needs a test first: can the Fenring's model and animations follow the player's movement, and can
every player see the change? Fallback: a wolf-skin buff with a glowing pelt and no model swap.

## More single items

- **Games:** Buster Sword (a Limit gauge for Braver or Omnislash) and Masamune (Final Fantasy VII);
  Leviathan Axe (God of War); Divine Rapier (dropped on death, anyone can take it), Blink Dagger,
  Aegis of the Immortal (one revive), Radiance, Battle Fury (Dota 2); Windforce, the Grandfather
  (Diablo); Frostmourne, Ashbringer, Thori'dal (needs no arrows), Doomhammer, Hearthstone (World of
  Warcraft); Mehrunes' Razor, Wabbajack, Ebony Blade (Skyrim); Rivers of Blood, Moonveil,
  Moonlight Greatsword (Elden Ring, Dark Souls); Shield of Chaos, Varatha, Heart-Seeking Bow
  (Hades).
- **Books:** Sting, Glamdring and Orcrist (glow near Fulings), the mithril shirt, the One Ring, the
  Phial of Galadriel, the Horn of Gondor, lembas (The Lord of the Rings); the Sword of Gryffindor
  (takes the element of its last kill) (Harry Potter); Mat's ashandarei and foxhead medallion,
  Callandor (The Wheel of Time); Stormbringer (Elric).
- **Myth:** Excalibur and its scabbard (no bleeding while worn); Mjölnir, Gungnir, Draupnir
  (Norse); the Monkey King's extending staff and Guan Yu's Green Dragon Crescent Blade (Chinese);
  Kusanagi and Muramasa (Japanese); the Nemean Lion's pelt, the Golden Fleece, Achilles' armour
  (Greek); Gáe Bolg and Fragarach (Irish).
- **Borderline (sci-fi source):** the razor (Red Rising), mantis blades (Cyberpunk 2077).
- **Upgrades to shipped items:** the Scroll of Hearth Portal's two ends could each show a snapshot of
  where they lead (the spot it was read, taken then; home, taken when you last slept). Not a live
  view: Valheim only loads the world near players.

## Known hard parts
- Slowing time can't work in multiplayer; Dead Eye needs another form.
- Changing the player's model (werewolf, a bat form) needs a test before planning a set around it.
