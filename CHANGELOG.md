# Changelog

Newest first. Your saved config keeps its old values when a default changes; where an entry says to reset
a section, delete it from `BepInEx/config/omm.valheim.mysticalweapons.cfg` to get the new defaults.

## 29 Oct 2026
- Fixed: Brotherhood set: an arrow, bolt or thrown weapon that lands after you switch to the Hidden Blades no longer
  counts as an assassination.

## 28 Oct 2026
- Fixed: Hearth Portal: a freshly cast portal no longer says "The portal is still settling" when you step in.

## 27 Oct 2026
- Added: Scroll of Hearth Portal (Diablo's Town Portal): opens a portal home to your bed. Haldor sells it after The
  Elder, or craft it at the Galdr table.

## 25 Oct 2026
- Added: Brotherhood set (Assassin's Creed) and the Hidden Blades.
- Changed: every recipe fits the crafting panel's four ingredient slots, boss trophy included. Reset the `Recipe`
  line in `[Anduril]`, `[Sylspear]`, `[Shardblade]` and `[ElderWand]`.
- Fixed: the Shardplate breastplate and greaves no longer show as a white silhouette.

## 23 Oct 2026
- Added: Warglaives of Azzinoth, and a new look for the full Shardplate set.
- Changed: new powers for Andúril, the Sylspear, the Cloak of Invisibility, the Elder Wand, the Bat'leth, Shardplate
  and the Deathly Hallows set; the strongest items need a boss trophy; the Elder Wand moves to the Deep North. Reset
  `[Anduril]`, `[Sylspear]`, `[Shardblade]`, `[Batleth]`, `[ElderWand]` and the three Shardplate sections.
