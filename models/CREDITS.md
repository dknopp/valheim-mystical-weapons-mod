## ShardplateHelm

This work is based on "Shardplate" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/shardplate-5a4fca1f665b469c9669f387aa879b34)
by Krajkoa (https://sketchfab.com/Krajkoa) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: extracted the helm and collar from the full suit, recentred and rescaled it to metres,
and merged the emissive texture into a single 1024px base colour texture.

## ShardplateBreastplate, ShardplateGreaves

Based on the same model by Krajkoa (CC-BY-4.0, see above).

Changes: split the suit into its plates (leaving out the scale skirt), refitted them region by region to Valheim's player skeleton
(different body proportions), assigned each plate to a bone, reduced the triangle count, and merged
each material's emissive texture into a 1024px base colour texture. Icons rendered from the result.
